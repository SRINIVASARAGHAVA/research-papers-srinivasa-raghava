## Paper summaries

### 1. Taylor coefficients and duality for finite multiple T-values

Uses coefficients of iterated integrals to represent finite multiple T-values. It develops Bernoulli convolution formulas and duality identities, including a specified family from Cheng and Zhao's conjecture.

[Read PDF (2611.23315v1)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.23315v1) · [arXiv record & version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23315) · [Details & citation](papers/2611.23315/)

### 2. Coefficient arithmetic and Mellin moments of the Rogers-Ramanujan continued fraction

Investigates signs and arithmetic properties of logarithmic coefficients of the Rogers-Ramanujan continued fraction. It relates these coefficients to Mellin moment generating functions and integrals involving zeta and L-functions.

[Read PDF (2610.10578v4)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.10578v4) · [arXiv record & version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10578) · [Details & citation](papers/2610.10578/)

### 3. Dedekind Eta Powers and Interlacing in Lambert Series Quotients

Studies zero and pole interlacing in Lambert series quotients formed from Euler's product. It connects the cubic case with total positivity and analyzes interpolation and boundary limits for general integer powers.

[Read PDF (2609.22346v2)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.22346v2) · [arXiv record & version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22346) · [Details & citation](papers/2609.22346/)

### 4. Exact Moments and CM Periods of Reciprocal Quadratic Mellin Integrals

Develops exact evaluations and recurrences for moments of reciprocal quadratic Mellin integrals. It also studies algebraic sampling functions and connections with elliptic curves, complex multiplication, and gamma-valued periods.

[Read PDF (2609.20201v2)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.20201v2) · [arXiv record & version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20201) · [Details & citation](papers/2609.20201/)

### 5. Radial Limits and Euler Exponents of a False Theta Series with Character Modulo 8

Examines a false theta series and its moment quotients through Dawson-integral transformations and radial limits at roots of unity. It also gives partition interpretations, Euler product exponent formulas, and information about zeros.

[Read PDF (2609.18956v2)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.18956v2) · [arXiv record & version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18956) · [Details & citation](papers/2609.18956/)

### 6. Arithmetic Constraints and Limit Laws for Diagonal Rational Partitions

Studies partitions into reduced positive fractions with bounded numerators and denominators. It develops growth asymptotics, arithmetic restrictions on prime-denominator contributions, and distributional results for residues and sampling.

[Read PDF (2609.18945v1)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.18945v1) · [arXiv record & version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18945) · [Details & citation](papers/2609.18945/)

### 7. Walsh eta-units at squarefree level-integral structure and Fricke values

Studies integral structures associated with modular units at squarefree levels and evaluates normalized units at Fricke points. Applications concern quadratic units, equations for inverse pi, and colored partition identities.

[Read PDF (2607.11926v2)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.11926v2) · [arXiv record & version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11926) · [Details & citation](papers/2607.11926/)

### 8. A Ramanujan Pell K3 Surface and Cube Representations

Classifies polynomial representations of a specified Ramanujan-Pell sextic as a sum of two cubes. It relates these representations to sections of an elliptic K3 surface and studies heights and denominator divisibility.

[Read PDF (2607.11925v4)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.11925v4) · [arXiv record & version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11925) · [Details & citation](papers/2607.11925/)

### 9. Harmonic weights in inverse central binomial series

Compares inverse central binomial series with different harmonic weights through their partial fraction coefficients. It gives uniform asymptotics, sharp sign conditions, Stieltjes function criteria, and identities at odd weights.

[Read PDF (2607.09904v3)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.09904v3) · [arXiv record & version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09904) · [Details & citation](papers/2607.09904/)

## Maintaining this collection

The public metadata is in [`docs/papers.json`](docs/papers.json). Check the individual arXiv records before changing titles, version numbers, dates, or PDF URLs. Keep submission dates distinct from version dates and label preprints accurately.

After updating the metadata, regenerate the catalogue with Python 3:

```sh
python scripts/build.py
```

The script creates the main index, paper detail pages, citations and static HTML. The website files are in [`docs/`](docs/) and are deployed by the GitHub Pages workflow. Search and filters run in the browser; all papers and citations remain readable without JavaScript.

PDF links currently point to the official version-specific arXiv files. The archive workflow can add checked copies in `docs/pdfs/` and rebuild the links to those copies. The builder selects a local PDF link only when the corresponding file is present. Archive only verified versions selected for public release.
